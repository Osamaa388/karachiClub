GOT QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the gym timings at Karachi Club GYM?

Karachi Club GYM runs dedicated morning and evening slots for men, with ladies-only hours reserved every afternoon. Reach out on WhatsApp or call the Nazimabad branch to confirm same-day slot availability.

What membership plans does Karachi Club GYM offer?

We offer flexible monthly and long-term membership plans covering gym access, personal training, and diet guidance. Pricing depends on the program you choose, so our team will walk you through options during your visit.

Do you offer a free trial before joining?

Yes, new visitors can book a free trial session to try the equipment, meet a trainer, and see the facility before committing to a membership plan at our Nazimabad gym.

Is the ladies-only section really private?

Yes, the ladies-only section runs during fixed afternoon hours with entry limited to female members and trainers, giving women a private, comfortable space to train without interruption.

Is Karachi Club GYM suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Our trainers start beginners on foundational strength and conditioning work, then progress the plan gradually as your fitness, confidence, and technique improve.

How do I book a personal training session?

You can book a personal training session by visiting our Nazimabad branch, calling our team, or messaging us on WhatsApp with your preferred day and time.

What gym equipment is available on the training floor?

Our floor includes upgraded strength machines, free weights, cardio equipment, and a dedicated area for HIIT and functional training, all maintained for daily use.
